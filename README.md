# Bitcoin-Review-of-Books
The Bitcoin Review of Books repository

https://bitcoinbooks.review/ 
Library of Congress ISSN 3068-7780

The Bitcoin Review of Books (BRB) is the free, open-source space for the review of books whose primary topic is Bitcoin.

The definition of “Bitcoin book” continues to evolve as Bitcoin Studies does likewise. As such, authors and publishers are welcome to submit any book related to Bitcoin to BRB for consideration.

BRB publishes reviews on a rolling basis with the intention of a review or review-set ~5000 blocks.

On how to submit a book for review or a review for publication, see instructions: https://bitcoinbooks.review/asm/

BRB is no-fees open-access. Books are reviewed and reviews are published based on their originality, clarity, accessibility, contribution to the field of Bitcoin Studies, relevance to Bitcoin in real time, and interest to BRB’s readership.

Authors maintain rights to their work per CC (creative commons) license.

BRB is sustained by community donations.
